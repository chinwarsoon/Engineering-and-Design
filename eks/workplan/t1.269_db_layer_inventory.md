## 5. Id Strategy (approved Q2)

- **Every table** gets `id UUID PK` — stored VARCHAR(36) in DuckDB, system-generated.
- **Definition tables**: deterministic `uuid5(namespace, natural_key)` where namespace = the `eks:<table>` value above. Deterministic ⇒ re-runs are idempotent (`INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING`), §24-stable, no duplicate rows.
- **Runtime data tables**: `uuid4()` at insert time (transient identity; natural key is the business data).
- Natural-key UNIQUE constraint retained on every definition table (e.g. `element_type UNIQUE`, `(project_code, local_code) UNIQUE`).

## 6. FK Policy (approved Q1)

- All 42 definition + 9 runtime tables materialized ⇒ physical `FOREIGN KEY` constraints can be emitted (DuckDB supports FK enforcement).
- `_eks_table_relations` declared-only manifest retired; its 30+ declared relationships become real physical FKs declared in `eks_db_config.json` `foreign_keys[]`.
- Load-order (above) is topological: every FK target precedes its referencing table.
